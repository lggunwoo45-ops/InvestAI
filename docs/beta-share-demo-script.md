# Market Copilot Beta Share Demo Script

## Purpose

This short script helps an internal presenter share the current beta consistently. The beta organizes already-loaded market evidence for review. It does not predict returns, recommend a transaction, or execute an order.

## Before sharing

- Confirm the Market, News, and DART data-state labels shown on screen.
- Explain that crypto may use public live data while stock catalogs remain mock/demo data.
- Use only the prepared demo routes and do not enter personal notes or account information.
- If a dependency is unavailable, keep the unavailable state visible. Do not describe fallback data as live.

## Five-minute flow

1. **Dashboard** — Introduce the market radar as a starting point for identifying areas to review.
2. **Market** — Select one instrument and show the source, current data state, chart, orderbook, and recent-trade boundaries.
3. **AI Analysis** — Choose a market bucket and explain that its interest candidate list is a fixed snapshot until the user refreshes it.
4. **Candidate review score** — Explain that the score summarizes current data quality, review basis, available ranges, and missing evidence. It is not a rank, return probability, or transaction signal.
5. **My Analysis** — Open one candidate and show the practical decision state, approximate review ranges, evidence, missing evidence, and the copyable safe summary.
6. **News / DART** — Show source evidence only when available and open the original source for interpretation.
7. **Demo** — Close with the readiness and limitation panels. Clearly name the features that are not connected.

## Required safety wording

> The review score and ranges are decision-support information. They are not profit probability, investment advice, or trade instructions.

## Korean presenter notes

1. 대시보드에서 시장 레이더가 검토 시작점임을 설명합니다.
2. 마켓에서 종목 하나를 선택하고 출처와 데이터 상태를 먼저 확인합니다.
3. AI Analysis의 관심 후보는 새로고침 전까지 고정된 기준 기록임을 설명합니다.
4. 검토 점수는 데이터 품질·판단 근거·검토 범위·누락 정보를 한눈에 확인하기 위한 참고값이며 순위나 수익 가능성이 아님을 설명합니다.
5. 내 종목 분석에서 지금 판단, 검토 범위, 확인된 근거와 부족한 근거, 복사 가능한 요약을 보여줍니다.
6. 뉴스와 DART는 원문 확인이 필요한 출처 근거이며 방향을 예측하지 않는다고 설명합니다.
7. 마지막으로 데모 화면에서 연결되지 않은 기능과 베타 한계를 분명히 알립니다.

필수 안내 문구:

> 검토 점수와 범위는 판단 보조 정보이며, 수익 가능성이나 투자 조언, 거래 지시가 아닙니다.

## Stop conditions

Stop the demo and explain the limitation if the displayed source state conflicts with the spoken description, the selected instrument has no usable review basis, or the presenter cannot verify whether data is live or mock.
